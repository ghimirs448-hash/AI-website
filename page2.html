Artificial Intelligence in Everyday Life

AI is already part of many activities we do every day.

How Do We Use AI?

Artificial intelligence is used in many applications and devices that people interact with every day. For example, smartphones use AI for facial recognition, voice assistants, photography, and text suggestions.

Streaming services and social media platforms also use AI. These systems analyze information about what users watch or interact with and use that information to recommend content that may interest them.

Examples of AI in Daily Life

Here are five common examples of AI:

  1. Using facial recognition to unlock a smartphone
  2. Asking a virtual assistant a question
  3. Receiving movie and music recommendations
  4. Using navigation apps to find the best route
  5. Using chatbots to get information or assistance

Why is AI Important?

AI can help people complete tasks more quickly and efficiently. It can analyze large amounts of information, automate repetitive work, and help people make better decisions.

As AI continues to develop, understanding how it works will become increasingly important. Learning about AI can help people understand both the opportunities and challenges created by this technology.
